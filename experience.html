My Experience

During my industrial attachment I gained practical exposure to networking, IT support and enterprise IT environments, and saw how classroom concepts are applied in a real organisation.

Workplace Experience

ICT Attachment Student

Kenya Ports Authority (KPA)

Position
ICT Attachment Student
Department / Area
ICT: Networking and IT Support
Duration
May 2026 – July 2026

I completed a hands-on industrial attachment in the ICT department, working alongside the networking and IT support teams. My work covered switch and VLAN configuration, network troubleshooting, end-user support and routine equipment maintenance.

Roles and Responsibilities

Networking

  • Assisted with configuring network switches and VLANs.
  • Gained practical experience with IP addressing and subnetting.
  • Used PuTTY and switch console access to configure and manage devices.
  • Assisted with troubleshooting network connectivity problems.
  • Worked with Ethernet cables, RJ45 termination and cable testing.
  • Worked with wireless networking equipment, including access points and repeaters.
  • Participated in network switch configuration and maintenance.

IT Support

  • Assisted with password resets and end-user support.
  • Helped troubleshoot network connectivity and IP phone issues.
  • Assisted with software installation and configuration.
  • Gained practical exposure to Windows installation, Microsoft Office, SAP and domain-joined computers.

Network Administration

  • Assisted with a VLAN setup requested by users in the fire department to support remote UPS monitoring.
  • Participated in troubleshooting a faulty switch that affected IP phone connectivity.
  • Assisted with switch-port changes and the restoration of connectivity.

Preventive Maintenance

  • Participated in equipment maintenance and basic hardware troubleshooting.
  • Assisted with IP phone maintenance and cleaning, using appropriate precautions.

Skills Gained

Networking and Infrastructure

Practical experience with switches, VLANs, IP addressing, subnetting and cabling, gained while configuring and maintaining the organisation's network equipment.

IT Support and Troubleshooting

Diagnosing connectivity and IP phone problems and supporting users with issues such as password resets, working step by step from symptom to cause.

Hardware and Software Configuration

Installing and configuring software, working with Windows, Microsoft Office, SAP and domain-joined computers, and handling networking hardware safely.

Communication and Teamwork

Working with ICT colleagues and listening to users' requirements, such as the fire department's request for remote UPS monitoring, then explaining what was done.

Problem-Solving and Technical Documentation

Isolating faults such as the faulty switch affecting IP phones, and recording configuration steps and changes clearly so that others can follow them.

Lessons Learned

The attachment showed me how enterprise networks depend on careful planning, consistent configuration and clear documentation. Seeing VLANs and switches support real users, such as phones and monitoring equipment, made the theory I learned in class much more concrete.

I also learned that good troubleshooting is methodical, and that teamwork and clear communication are as important as technical knowledge. Working in a live environment taught me to be careful with changes that affect other people, and I now see more clearly how classroom learning connects to real-world IT operations.
